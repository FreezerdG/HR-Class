The Schedule

9:00 AM

Welcome & Introduction

HR / People Ops

Warm welcome and company overview. Mission, vision, and values — told through stories, not slides. Who's who across leadership and key contacts. Office tour.

10:00 AM

Administrative & Compliance

HR

I-9 verification (must be completed Day 1). W-4 and state tax forms. Direct deposit setup. Policy acknowledgments — handbook, code of conduct, acceptable use. Benefits overview and enrollment timeline.

Informal, relationship-building. No work agenda — just good food and good company.

Buddy Introduction

4:30 PM
